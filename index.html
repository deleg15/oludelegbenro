About me
Brand and product leadership.
Introduction

I'm Oludele Samson Gbenro — a Lagos-based Head of Product Management and Brand Communication at First Registrars & Investor Services Limited, and Brand Experience Manager at CleCheck, a KYC and identity verification company. At First Registrars, what looks like loyalty is really the story of someone who kept growing, taking on new challenges, and redefining what in-house brand and product leadership looks like.

Mission

To bring together the discipline of brand thinking, the craft of visual design, and the speed of AI-assisted production — so that the organisations I work with can show up with confidence in every market they enter.

Career background

Started as a Graphic Designer at First Registrars & Investor Services Limited in 2008, progressed through Email Marketing Design and Brand Management, and now serve as Head of Product Management and Brand Communication. I also currently serve as Brand Experience Manager at CleCheck, a KYC and identity verification company. Along the way I added video production, AI tool integration, social media strategy, and cross-functional team leadership — without ever losing sight of the craft.

MBA, Ajayi Crowther University (2019), pursued for leadership development. Recognised as First Registrars Corporate Image Ambassador 2025.
