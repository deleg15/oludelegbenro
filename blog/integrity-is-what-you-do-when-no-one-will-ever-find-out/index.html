Integrity is what you do when no one will ever find out

It is easy to lead well on stage.

In the town hall.

In front of the board.

When the customer is watching.

The real test happens when nobody is.

Here's the truth:

Integrity is what you do when no one will ever find out.

Do you correct the invoice that overcharged a customer, even when they did not notice?

Do you give credit to the team member who did the work, even in a meeting they are not in?

Do you keep the promise that would be easy to quietly forget?

Your team may not see every one of those moments.

But they always feel the pattern.

Trust is not built by what you say in public.

It is built by who you are in private, repeated over time.

What is one small "unseen" choice that shows your character this week?
