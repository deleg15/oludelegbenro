Your calendar shows your real priorities

Open your calendar for next week.

What do you see?

Back-to-back meetings.

Reports.

Calls with clients.

Now look again.

Where are your people?

Here's the truth:

Your calendar shows your real priorities, not the ones you announce.

Many leaders say "my team is my greatest asset".

Then they never schedule time to sit with them.

No one-on-ones.

No coaching.

No time to ask how they are really doing.

Anything that matters must be given time.

So put it in the diary.

Thirty minutes with someone on your team.

A walk around the floor.

A conversation with no agenda.

People notice where you spend your hours.

Who needs a slot in your calendar next week?
