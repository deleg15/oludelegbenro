Clarity is kindness

New week. New tasks. New expectations.

Before you hand any of them out, ask yourself one question:

Does my team know exactly what "done" looks like?

Many leaders think they are being nice by keeping things vague.

"Just handle it."

"Do your best."

"Get it to me soon."

Then they are disappointed with the result.

Here's the truth:

Clarity is kindness. Unclear expectations are unfair to good people.

Be clear about the outcome.

Be clear about the deadline, a day and a time, not "soon".

Be clear about the standard and who to ask for help.

Clear does not mean harsh.

It means you respect people enough to set them up to win.

Most performance problems I see in SMEs start as clarity problems.

What is one task you could make clearer for your team this Monday?
