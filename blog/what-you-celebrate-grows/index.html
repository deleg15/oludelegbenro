What you celebrate, grows

It's Friday, a good day to ask a simple question:

What did we celebrate this week?

Many teams only celebrate the big numbers.

The record sale.

The new contract.

The target hit.

Those matter.

But if that is all you applaud, people learn that only results count, however they were achieved.

Here's the truth:

What you celebrate, grows.

Celebrate the staff member who calmed an angry customer.

Celebrate the person who raised a problem early.

Celebrate the colleague who helped someone else finish on time.

Celebrate honesty, ownership and care, not just the scoreboard.

Your team is always watching what earns your attention.

Make sure it is the behaviour you want to see again on Monday.

Who on your team deserves a specific "thank you" before the weekend?
