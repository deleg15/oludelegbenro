Customer experience starts in the staff room

Your customers will never read your values statement.

They will meet your team.

And your team will treat them the way you treat your team.

If you are dismissive in meetings, do not expect warmth at the front desk.

If you ignore complaints from staff, do not expect patience with customers.

If you only appear when something goes wrong, do not expect pride in the work.

Here's the truth:

Customer experience starts in the staff room.

A team that feels heard, respected and supported finds it natural to do the same for customers.

A team that feels ignored will pass that feeling on, one transaction at a time.

You cannot demand a smile you have not earned.

Want better service?

Start by serving the people who serve your customers.

How would your team describe the way you treat them?
