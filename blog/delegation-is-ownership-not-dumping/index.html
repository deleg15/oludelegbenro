Delegation is ownership, not dumping

There is a difference between delegating and dumping.

Dumping sounds like:

"Take this. I don't have time."

No context. No authority. And then you take it back the moment it is not done your way.

Delegating sounds like:

"Here is the result we need, here is why it matters, and you have the authority to decide how."

Here's the truth:

Delegation is handing over ownership, not just a task.

Share the outcome, not only the steps.

Give the authority to make decisions, not just the work.

Agree on check-ins, then step back.

And when they do it differently from you, ask first whether it is wrong or just different.

People grow when they own something.

They shrink when they only carry what you did not want.

What is one responsibility you could truly hand over this week?
