Lead yourself first

Before you lead a team, a department or a business, you lead one person.

Yourself.

How you manage your time.

How you handle pressure.

How you speak when things go wrong.

Here's the truth:

You cannot take your team further than you have taken yourself.

Your people do not only listen to your instructions.

They watch your habits.

If you arrive late, deadlines become suggestions.

If you shout under pressure, they learn to hide problems.

If you keep learning, they start learning too.

Self-leadership is not glamorous.

It is the quiet work of keeping your own promises before you ask others to keep theirs.

Over the next six weeks, I'll share one leadership lesson a day, all the way to the end of the year.

We start here, with the person in the mirror.

What is one habit you want to lead yourself on before the year ends?
