The best leaders speak last

In many meetings, the most senior person speaks first.

Then everyone else agrees.

Quietly.

Politely.

And the real concerns stay unspoken.

Here's the truth:

The best leaders listen more than they talk.

They speak last, so people feel free to speak honestly.

They ask a question, then wait through the silence.

They notice who has gone quiet.

They listen for what is not being said: the hesitation, the worry, the idea nobody dared to raise.

Listening is not passive.

It is the fastest way to learn what is really happening in your business.

And it tells people that their voice matters.

In your next meeting, try this: ask, then say nothing for ten seconds.

You may be surprised by what you hear.

When last did you leave a meeting having talked less than you listened?
